Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

QuickCart Inc. ("QuickCart", "we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use the QuickCart mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Details: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Location Data: Geolocation coordinates (GPS) with your permission to enable 10-15 minute grocery routing, real-time live radar tracking, and dispatching local dark stores.
  • Order & Transaction History: Items ordered, payment method chosen (COD, Card, UPI), delivery status, and timestamps.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, network connection status, and app performance logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your grocery orders in real-time.
  • To provide live radar tracking of your assigned delivery partner.
  • To verify coupons and offer customized discounts.
  • To communicate order status updates, delivery notifications, and customer support messages.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary delivery information (e.g., delivery address and items) with assigned delivery partners to fulfill your orders. All network traffic is encrypted using HTTPS and secure WebSocket protocols.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our privacy officer.
